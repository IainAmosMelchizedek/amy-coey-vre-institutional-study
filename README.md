# While She Managed My Rehabilitation, I Studied the Institution Through Her

## Amy Coey, VR&E, and a Four-Dimensional Study of Institutional Power

This repository preserves my relationship with **Amy Coey**, the Veterans Benefits Administration (VBA) Vocational Rehabilitation Counselor assigned to me through the **Veteran Readiness and Employment (VR&E)** program.

It will house correspondence, VR&E records, rehabilitation plans, case-status documents, contemporaneous notes, legal and regulatory analysis, personal recollections, and my continuing examination of this relationship through the same four-dimensional framework I formally articulated in 2019:

> **political, social, religious, and economic reasons**

This repository concerns Amy specifically.

It is the record of what occurred between **me and Amy Coey**, the federal program she administered in relation to me, the decisions and representations made during that relationship, and what I observed while moving through it.

---

## The Relationship Began One Day After the OIG Raid

The chronology matters.

On **June 13, 2023**, agents of the Office of Inspector General entered and searched my residence in connection with what I reject as a false data-breach narrative arising after my whistleblower activity at the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau.

On **June 14, 2023**, one day later, I was interviewed by Amy Coey to determine my eligibility for participation in the VR&E program.

During that interview, she asked whether I had a criminal history.

I told her that I did not.

I also told her that my residence had been raided the previous day and explained the broader whistleblower and federal-conflict context as I understood it.

That timing is foundational to this archive.

Amy did not encounter me in an ordinary period of my life.

She entered my life at the beginning of an extraordinary period in which I was simultaneously dealing with the consequences of whistleblowing, federal accusation, institutional conflict, and an attempt to rebuild my professional future.

The VR&E relationship therefore began at the intersection of **rehabilitation, federal power, livelihood, institutional vulnerability, and survival**.

## Rehabilitation, Autonomy, and Institutional Control

VR&E is presented as a rehabilitative program.

My experience with Amy increasingly raised a different question:

> **Who actually controls rehabilitation—the veteran whose life is being rebuilt, or the institution administering the benefit?**

That question became particularly important when I began discussing self-employment and the company I founded, **Safe Passage Strategies, LLC**.

My position was that I could pursue multiple lawful avenues of suitable employment simultaneously, including both traditional job seeking and entrepreneurial development.

I repeatedly sought clarity regarding the legal and regulatory basis for limitations being asserted against me.

My affidavit documents my view that critical information concerning self-employment had not been disclosed to me at the outset of the rehabilitation process, and that requirements were introduced only after I independently raised self-employment as a vocational path.

The resulting dispute became about far more than a business.

It became a dispute over:

- who defines successful rehabilitation;
- who defines suitable employment;
- whether a veteran may pursue multiple economic paths;
- whether institutional categories can override lived economic reality;
- whether procedure exists to help the veteran or to control the veteran; and
- what happens when the individual being administered refuses to surrender interpretive authority over his own life.

## The Four-Dimensional Inquiry

### Political

Amy was not merely an individual counselor.

She exercised delegated authority inside a federal institution.

I examine:

- the political nature of administrative authority;
- the relationship between counselor discretion and federal policy;
- the power to classify, approve, delay, condition, or close a veteran's rehabilitation case;
- the use of internal rules and administrative categories;
- the treatment of dissent and self-advocacy inside government systems;
- the broader political environment in which this relationship occurred; and
- the relationship between my prior federal whistleblower conflict and my later experience inside another federal institution.

When I describe Amy as a **political operative**, I use that term in my own analytical sense: a government employee exercising institutional authority within a political system.

I am not using the term to allege that she held a campaign position, belonged to a political organization, or acted pursuant to a covert partisan assignment unless evidence independently establishes such a fact.

My present characterization of her conduct as ideological, nationalist, cult-like, or aligned with a political administration is part of my own interpretation and will remain clearly distinguished from independently verifiable evidence.

### Social

I examine the social structure surrounding the VR&E relationship.

That includes:

- counselor and veteran;
- administrator and beneficiary;
- professional and client;
- credentialed official and person being evaluated;
- institutional insider and institutional subject;
- social status;
- class;
- hierarchy;
- credibility;
- professional socialization;
- dependency;

- the expectations imposed on veterans receiving government benefits.

I also examine veteran status as a social category.

Military service is often publicly honored while veterans themselves may occupy materially vulnerable positions after service.

My inquiry asks what happens when the institution that publicly claims to honor veterans also possesses substantial power over their education, livelihood, rehabilitation, healthcare, records, and economic future.

I reject the idea that a veteran ceases to be a private citizen merely because that veteran receives services from the government.

This repository will examine the social consequences of that institutional relationship.

### Religious

I entered my relationship with Amy with a spiritual framework that preceded both her and the 2023 federal conflict.

I assert that I have operated from the **Order of Melchizedek** since 2019.

My spiritual authority does not originate in the VBA, the VA, psychology, vocational rehabilitation, administrative law, federal employment, or the United States Government.

This dimension examines what occurs when institutional authority encounters a person who does not recognize that institution as the highest source of truth.

I also examine what I describe as the **religious or cult-like qualities of institutional life**:

- ritualized procedure;
- loyalty to internal categories;

When I use the term **cult** or **cult member**, I am describing my interpretation of these institutional dynamics. I am not presenting a psychiatric or clinical diagnosis of Amy Coey.

## Archival Principle

Preserve chronology.

Whenever possible, filenames should begin with the date and time.

Examples:

```text
YYYY-MM-DD_HHMM_Subject_Amy-Coey.pdf
YYYY-MM-DD_HHMM_to_YYYY-MM-DD_HHMM_Subject_Amy-Coey-Thread.pdf
YYYY-MM-DD_VRE-Event_Amy-Coey_Notes.md
YYYY-MM-DD_Affidavit-or-Declaration_Amy-Coey.pdf
```

Do not invent missing dates or times.

Retrospective notes should identify both:

- the date the note was written; and
- the date of the event being remembered, including uncertainty when necessary.

The archive should remain readable as a timeline.
